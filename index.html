About

Jen Hilman

A dancer, choreographer, yogi and licensed massage therapist from Austin, Texas. Her grandmother taught dance; Jen grew up in the studio, took a BA in Dance, and found yoga in her final year of college. She moved to Austin for her Texas massage licence, and, wanting to give her clients more to take home, went on to her 200-hour yoga teacher training and her first Thai Yoga Massage study.

From 2010 to 2021 she taught millions of people through Psychetruth and her own channel. What sets her apart is the detail: she explains alignment the way a bodyworker would, because she is one. For anyone in chronic pain or coming back from injury, that patience is the whole point.

Every practice here is free and plays from YouTube: from Jen's own channel and from the channels she taught for, Psychetruth and YOGATX.
